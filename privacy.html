Privacy Policy

What this meeting costs — Apple Watch app · Last updated 3 August 2026

This app collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking. Your rate and your receipts stay on your Apple Watch and are never sent anywhere.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers is stored on your watch, in the app's own storage:

  • the pay rate you entered, its currency, period and working-week settings;
  • your receipts — the amount, duration, room size and target of each finished session;
  • the meeting presets you saved — a name, a room size and an optional target;
  • display and haptics preferences;
  • whether the one-time purchase has been unlocked, cached so the app works offline;
  • a small snapshot of the running session, shared with the app's own watch-face complication so it can draw the amount.

None of this leaves the device. It is not backed up to any service we operate, because we operate none.

Network use

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only network traffic is the App Store's, and only when you buy the one-time unlock, leave a tip, or restore a previous purchase. Apple performs those transactions; we never see your payment details, your Apple Account, or anything else about you.

Nothing about a tip is stored on the device or anywhere else. It unlocks nothing, so there is no state to keep — the app does not remember whether you left one, and cannot tell you.

Third parties

There are none. The app includes no third-party code, no software development kits, no advertising and no analytics libraries.

Tracking

The app does not track you across apps or websites, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not fingerprint your device. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Two ways, both immediate and both entirely under your control:

  • in the app, open Settings → Data and delete all receipts — or erase everything, which also removes the rate, the presets and every preference and returns the app to its first-launch screen;
  • delete the app from your Apple Watch, which removes everything it stored.

Because nothing is transmitted, there is no copy anywhere for us to delete on your behalf, and no request you need to send us.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something this page does not describe, this page changes first. The date at the top is the date of the most recent change.
