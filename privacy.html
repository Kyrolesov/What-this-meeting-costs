Privacy Policy

What this meeting costs — Apple Watch app · Last updated 14 August 2026

This app records one thing about you: purchases. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your rate and your receipts stay on your Apple Watch and are never sent anywhere. The single exception — an anonymous record of a purchase — is described below.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers is stored on your watch, in the app's own storage:

  • the pay rate you entered, its currency, period and working-week settings;
  • your receipts — the amount, duration, room size and target of each finished session;
  • the meeting presets you saved — a name, a room size and an optional target;
  • display and haptics preferences;
  • whether the one-time purchase has been unlocked, cached so the app works offline;
  • a small snapshot of the running session, shared with the app's own watch-face complication so it can draw the amount.

None of this leaves the device. It is not backed up to any service we operate, because we operate none.

Network use

The app touches the network in exactly one situation: a purchase. When you buy the one-time unlock, leave a tip, or restore a previous purchase, Apple performs the transaction through the App Store; we never see your payment details, your Apple Account, or anything else about you.

After a purchase, the app reports it to RevenueCat, a purchase-records service we use to see sales totals. The report contains the transaction — product, price, currency, date — and a random identifier the app generates for its own installation. It does not contain your name, your Apple Account, your rate, your receipts, or anything you typed. If their service is unreachable, the app behaves exactly the same.

A tip is reported the same way, and it still unlocks nothing — the app does not remember whether you left one, and cannot tell you.

Third parties

One: RevenueCat, which receives the purchase records described above and nothing else. Purchases themselves are verified and delivered by Apple — RevenueCat is bookkeeping, not a gatekeeper. There is no advertising and no analytics library.

Tracking

The app does not track you across apps or websites, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not fingerprint your device. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking. In the App Store privacy label, the purchase records appear as Purchase History, not linked to your identity.

Children

The app is rated 4+. The only thing it ever records about anyone, of any age, is an anonymous purchase.

Deleting your data

Two ways, both immediate and both entirely under your control:

  • in the app, open Settings → Data and delete all receipts — or erase everything, which also removes the rate, the presets and every preference and returns the app to its first-launch screen;
  • delete the app from your Apple Watch, which removes everything it stored.

Your rate, receipts and presets exist nowhere but the watch, so there is no copy of them for us to delete on your behalf. The purchase records held by RevenueCat are anonymous and not linked to your name or Apple Account; if you want them gone anyway, write to the address below and we will request their deletion.

Changes to this policy
