What this meeting costs — Apple Watch app · Last updated 18 October 2026

Set your pay rate, start the counter, and watch the money burn instead of the clock. It is a joke told with arithmetic — not a time tracker, and it will not help you bill anybody.

Requirements

  • Apple Watch running watchOS 11 or later.
  • No iPhone app is needed, and there isn't one — everything happens on the watch.

Common questions

Does the counter keep running when I lower my wrist?

Yes. The app does not count ticks — it stores the moment the session started and works out the amount from the difference whenever it draws. Backgrounding the app, letting the screen sleep, or even force-quitting does not lose time or money.

A short session disappeared. Is that a bug?

No. Sessions under one minute are not recorded, and the app says so on screen instead of returning silently to the start. Below a minute the amount is too small to be the joke, and a history full of those is a history nobody opens.

What is free, and what is paid?

The free app is a complete single-person timer with no session limit, absurd equivalents and the summary after every session, plus your three most recent receipts. Starting a session is never blocked.

The one-time purchase adds the room mode for more than one person, the burn target with its escalating haptics, meeting presets, the full receipt history, the today / week / month totals with your most expensive session so far, larger equivalents for expensive meetings, and the live amount on your watch face. There is no subscription and no account.

What is the Thank you button in Settings?

A tip, and nothing more. It unlocks no feature, adds no badge and changes nothing in the app — if it did, it would be a purchase rather than a thank you. You never have to press it, and the app will not ask you to. There is nothing to restore, because there is nothing to get back.

I bought it and it shows as locked.

Open Settings → Pro and tap Restore purchase. Make sure the watch is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. The unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so it never expires.

Can I change my rate later?

Yes, in Settings → Rate. Past receipts keep the rate they ran on — editing the rate never rewrites history.

The amount looks wrong.

Check the accrual basis in Settings → Rate. A monthly salary divided by working time and the same salary divided by calendar time are two very different per-second rates, and the difference is more than fourfold. The confirmation screen during setup shows what your entry works out to per hour and per second.

The watch-face complication only shows a flame.

Without the one-time purchase, that is all it does: it opens the app. The live amount on the watch face is part of the purchase.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, Settings → Data deletes all receipts, or erases everything and returns the app to its first screen. Deleting the app from the watch removes everything too. Nothing is stored anywhere else — see the privacy policy.

Why is there no iPhone app?

Because there would be nothing for it to do. The point is showing somebody your wrist.
